# About Us

Points & Miles Calculator is an independent, free toolkit designed to help frequent flyers and travelers maximize the value of their loyalty rewards. 

The world of points and miles is notoriously opaque. Airlines and hotels frequently change their award charts, introduce dynamic pricing, and mask the true cost of redemptions with exorbitant fuel surcharges. We built this platform because we were tired of using spreadsheets to figure out if we were getting a good deal.

## Our Mission

Our mission is simple: **To prevent you from wasting your hard-earned points on terrible redemptions.**

We do this by providing:
- Free, client-side calculators that account for hidden costs like taxes and forgone earnings.
- Objective market valuations based on industry consensus.
- Educational guides to help beginners understand the math behind travel hacking.

## Transparency and Independence

- **No Affiliation**: We are entirely independent. We are not owned by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any airline, hotel group, or credit card issuer.
- **Privacy First**: All calculations run entirely locally in your web browser. We do not transmit, collect, or store the numbers you enter into our calculators. 
- **Editorial Integrity**: The valuations and opinions expressed on this site are our own editorial estimates. They are not influenced by affiliate partnerships.

If you find our tools helpful, please share them with your fellow travelers. Safe travels and happy redeeming!

## Brand Identity

We operate globally under the name **Points & Miles Calculator** and serve the Chinese-speaking community under our alternate name **里程账** (Li Cheng Zhang). Both versions of the site share the same underlying logic, commitment to privacy, and independent editorial standards.
