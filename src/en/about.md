# About Points & Miles Calculator

Points & Miles Calculator is an independent, free toolkit created and maintained by **peter**, a passionate frequent flyer enthusiast and software engineer. Our suite of calculators is designed to help everyday travelers cut through the marketing noise, evaluate dynamic award pricing, and maximize the true value of their credit card rewards and airline miles.

## The Problem: Why We Built This Platform

The world of airline loyalty programs and credit card rewards has grown increasingly convoluted. In recent years, major carriers and hotel chains have aggressively dismantled transparent award charts in favor of opaque dynamic pricing models. In many cases, redemption costs fluctuate wildly from day to day, and airlines frequently tack on exorbitant carrier-imposed fuel surcharges (YQ/YR) that can cost hundreds of dollars on top of your hard-earned points.

Compounding this problem is the affiliate marketing landscape. Many popular credit card and travel websites rely heavily on high-commission affiliate links. To entice readers into signing up for lucrative financial products, publishers often quote inflated, unrealistic point valuations—such as claiming transferable points are routinely worth 2.0+ cents each. While high valuations may be achievable for flexible solo travelers booking last-minute international first-class cabins, everyday travelers flying economy or traveling with family during peak holiday seasons often realize far less value—sometimes redeeming points at an abysmal 0.8 to 1.1 cents each.

Frustrated by maintaining cumbersome spreadsheets and constantly recalculating taxes, ticket comparisons, and opportunity costs by hand, peter developed Points & Miles Calculator as a public utility. Our objective is straightforward: **bring radical transparency and pure mathematics to travel hacking so that you never waste points or overpay for an award ticket.**

## How We Calculate Baseline Valuations

The reference point valuations across our platform are not arbitrary numbers or speculative ceilings. Instead, they reflect realistic, market-grounded benchmarks derived from real redemption scenarios:

1. **Median Real-World Utility**: We evaluate typical redemption availability across major routes (transpacific, transatlantic, and domestic hubs) in both economy and premium cabins, avoiding cherry-picked extreme outliers.
2. **Net Savings Calculation**: When calculating cents-per-point (CPP), we insist on deducting all unavoidable cash outlay—including government taxes, security fees, and fuel surcharges. Furthermore, our calculators factor in the cash flight miles you forfeit by choosing an award ticket instead of a paid fare.
3. **Continuous Review**: Because loyalty program sweet spots shift and transfer partnerships evolve, we audit our benchmarks on a monthly basis and display verification dates on our valuation pages. You can read our detailed mathematical models and redemption assumptions on our [Valuation Methodology page](/en/methodology/).

## Our Core Commitments

To ensure absolute objectivity and maintain the trust of our users, Points & Miles Calculator operates under three uncompromising principles:

- **100% Independent — Zero Affiliate Commissions**: We do not participate in credit card affiliate programs, we do not earn referral bonuses from bank sign-ups, and we accept zero sponsored coverage from airlines or hotels. Our calculations are governed strictly by arithmetic, not commercial partnerships. If a transfer partner is unfavorable or an airline devalues its award chart, our data reflects that reality immediately.
- **Complete Client-Side Privacy**: All calculations run entirely locally in your web browser using lightweight client-side JavaScript. We do not maintain any user database, and we never transmit, collect, or store the ticket prices, point balances, or personal valuations you enter into our tools.
- **Honest Simplicity**: We present tools as they are—without fabricated user reviews, fake expert personas, or misleading marketing claims.
