# Transfer Bonus Calculator Guide

Transfer bonuses are one of the best ways to stretch the value of your credit card points. Programs like Chase Ultimate Rewards, Amex Membership Rewards, and Capital One Miles frequently offer promotions to transfer points to airlines or hotels with an added bonus.

## How Transfer Bonuses Work

During a promotional period, a bank will add a percentage multiplier when you transfer your points to a specific airline or hotel partner. 

### Examples of Common Bonuses
* **15% Bonus:** Transfer 1,000 points, receive 1,150 miles.
* **20% Bonus:** Transfer 1,000 points, receive 1,200 miles.
* **25% Bonus:** Transfer 1,000 points, receive 1,250 miles.
* **30% Bonus:** Transfer 1,000 points, receive 1,300 miles.

## The Base Ratio

Not all points transfer at a 1:1 ratio to begin with. Always check the base transfer ratio before calculating the bonus. For example, Marriott Bonvoy points typically transfer to airlines at a 3:1 ratio. A bonus would be applied on top of that base ratio.

## Minimum Increments

Banks usually require you to transfer points in minimum increments, often 1,000 points. You cannot transfer exactly 1,234 points; you would have to transfer 2,000 points.

## Rounding Up

Because you have to transfer in fixed increments, you often end up transferring slightly more points than you actually need for your redemption. Keep this in mind when running the math.

## One-Way Transfers

Remember that transfers are strictly a one-way street. Once you move your points from your credit card to an airline or hotel, you **cannot** move them back. 

## No Speculative Transfers

Because transfers are irreversible, **never transfer points speculatively**. Do not move your points just because there is a 30% bonus. Only transfer points when you have found award availability and are ready to book immediately.
