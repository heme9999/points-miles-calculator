# How to Maximize Free Night Awards (FNAs)
"
If you hold a co-branded hotel credit card from Marriott, Hilton, or IHG, you likely receive a Free Night Award (FNA) every year upon card renewal. While they sound straightforward, "free" nights come with rules, caps, and expiration dates.

## What is a Free Night Award?

A Free Night Award is a certificate that can be redeemed for a one-night stay at participating properties within a hotel portfolio. 

- **Marriott Bonvoy**: Certificates are capped by point value (e.g., 35,000 points or 50,000 points). However, Marriott allows you to "top off" your certificate with up to 15,000 of your own points to book a more expensive property.
- **Hilton Honors**: Hilton's Free Night Rewards are uncapped, meaning they can be used at almost any property in the world (with a few exclusions) on any day of the week, provided there is a standard room reward available.

## How to Earn Them

The easiest way to earn FNAs is by holding premium hotel credit cards. Many cards offer them as an anniversary perk. You can also earn them by hitting specific spending thresholds or through promotional offers.

## Best Uses and Strategies

1. **Aim for High Cash Prices**: Since a certificate covers the room regardless of the cash price (up to the point cap), use it when cash prices are unusually high due to events, holidays, or peak season.
2. **Watch the Expiration**: FNAs typically expire 12 months from the date of issuance. They rarely can be extended, so use them before you lose them.
3. **Avoid Using Them on Cheap Nights**: If a hotel costs $100 but requires a 50k certificate, pay cash instead and save the certificate for a higher-value redemption.
