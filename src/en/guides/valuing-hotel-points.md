# Valuing Hotel Points

Unlike airline miles, which can yield wildly different values depending on the cabin class you fly, hotel point valuations tend to be more constrained. However, there are still major factors that can significantly alter the value you get from a hotel redemption.

## Dynamic Pricing vs. Award Charts

Historically, hotel programs used fixed award charts (e.g., a Category 5 hotel always cost 35,000 points). Today, major programs like Marriott and Hilton have shifted to **dynamic pricing**, where the point cost fluctuates based on the cash price of the room.

While dynamic pricing reduces the occurrence of outsized, astronomical redemptions, it guarantees a more consistent baseline value for your points.

## The 5th Night Free Trick

One of the most powerful benefits of hotel loyalty programs is the "5th Night Free" perk on award stays:
- **Marriott**: Available to all members booking a 5-night award stay.
- **Hilton**: Available to members with Silver elite status or higher.

When you book 5 consecutive nights on points, you only pay for 4. This effectively gives you a 20% discount on the total point cost, significantly boosting your cents-per-point (CPP) value.

## The Impact of Resort Fees

When calculating the value of your points, you must factor in taxes and resort fees:
- **Hilton & Hyatt**: Completely waive resort fees on award stays. This can save you $30-$50 per night and greatly increases the value of your points.
- **Marriott**: Does **not** waive resort fees on award stays (except at a few specific properties). You still have to pay them out of pocket.
