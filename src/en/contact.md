# Contact Us

We’re always looking for ways to improve the Points & Miles Calculator. Whether you’ve found a bug, want to suggest a new calculator feature, or think a valuation needs updating, we’d love to hear from you.

## How to Reach Us

Please send us an email at **hello@points-miles-calculator.com** to provide feedback or report bugs.

**Note:** As a free tool, we cannot provide personalized travel consulting, credit card recommendations, or step-by-step award booking assistance. 

Thank you for your support and for helping us make this toolkit better for the frequent flyer community!
