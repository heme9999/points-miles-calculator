## Expected Response Time

As an independently maintained passion project, all incoming emails are reviewed personally. You can typically expect a response within **24 to 48 hours** on business days (replies may take slightly longer during holidays or travel periods).

## Reporting Valuation Updates and Technical Issues

Loyalty program rules, transfer bonuses, and carrier surcharges change frequently. If you notice that an airline valuation no longer aligns with current market realities, or if you encounter an unexpected calculation result, please let us know! To help us investigate and patch updates quickly, please consider including:

- **Program Name**: The specific loyalty program or bank transferable currency involved (e.g., Amex Membership Rewards, United MileagePlus, Air Canada Aeroplan).
- **Official Source**: A link to the program's official announcement, updated terms and conditions, or published award chart.
- **Concrete Example**: Origin/destination airport codes, dates, cabin class, cash ticket price, required miles, and taxes/fees.
- **Mathematical Rationale**: The calculated cents-per-point (CPP) figure or suggested valuation adjustment.

- **No Personalized Award Booking or Travel Consulting**: Points & Miles Calculator is an educational, self-service math tool. Due to time constraints, we cannot offer personalized trip planning, custom itinerary searching, or individual credit card advice.
