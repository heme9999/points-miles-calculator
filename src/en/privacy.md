# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Points & Miles Calculator handles your information.

## 1. Local Processing (No Data Collection)

The core feature of this website—our calculators—operates entirely via client-side JavaScript. 
- **All calculations are performed locally within your web browser.** 
- We do not transmit, upload, or store the numbers, points balances, or financial data you enter into our tools on any server.
- We do not require you to create an account or log in to use our services.

## 2. Analytics and Tracking

We currently **do not use any analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party monitoring services** on this website. If we decide to implement privacy-respecting analytics in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

## 3. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. If any cookies are set by the infrastructure hosting our static site (such as Cloudflare Pages), they are strictly necessary for security and network performance.

## 4. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party websites (such as airlines, banks, and travel blogs). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of those external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any website you visit.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Your continued use of the site after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new Privacy Policy.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please [contact us](/en/contact/).
