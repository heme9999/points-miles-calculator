# Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: November 2026*

At Points & Miles Calculator, your privacy and data security are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy details our data handling practices, explains our client-side software architecture, and outlines our policies regarding cookies and third-party advertising partners across both our English and Chinese editions.

---

## 1. 100% Client-Side Processing (Zero Data Collection)

We designed our platform with strict privacy-by-design principles:
- **Local Browser Calculation**: All calculations in our tool suite—including the Cents-per-Point (CPP) Calculator, Points vs. Cash Redemption Tool, Transfer Partner Calculator, and Buy Points Break-Even Tool—are performed entirely on your device using client-side JavaScript.
- **No Data Transmission**: The numbers you enter (such as ticket prices, award mileage balances, personal valuations, or preferred currencies) are never transmitted to our servers or saved in a remote database. We do not operate a backend database that logs user inputs.
- **No Account Required**: You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal credentials to use any calculator on our site. Once you close or refresh your browser, temporary input numbers are cleared.

---

## 2. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies

To support the ongoing hosting, domain registration, and maintenance of Points & Miles Calculator as a free public resource, we may display online advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense. In compliance with Google AdSense program policies, we disclose the following practices regarding cookies:

- **Third-Party Vendor Cookies**: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the Internet.
- **DoubleClick DART Cookie**: Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve targeted, relevant ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other destinations on the web.
- **Opting Out of Personalized Advertising**: You have the right to control your ad preferences. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

---

## 3. Cookies and Local Storage

- **Client-Side Preferences (LocalStorage)**: Our website may utilize standard browser `localStorage` solely to remember user-selected preferences, such as your preferred currency display (e.g., USD, CAD, EUR) or dark/light display settings. This data resides strictly on your local device and is never synchronized with an external server.
- **Infrastructure & Security Cookies**: Points & Miles Calculator is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may deploy strictly necessary technical cookies (such as security tokens) to protect our website against distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks and malicious automated traffic. These technical cookies do not track personal identifying information.

---

## 4. Links to External Websites

Our articles, valuations, and transfer guides contain hyperlinks to third-party web destinations, including airline booking portals, bank reward programs, and travel forums. Once you click an external link and navigate away from Points & Miles Calculator, you are subject to the privacy practices and terms of service of that respective third-party website. We have no control over and assume no liability for the content or privacy practices of external websites.

---

## 5. Compliance with GDPR and CCPA

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):
- We do not collect, monetize, rent, or sell Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
- We do not build individual marketing profiles, track cross-site user identities, or store sensitive financial credentials.
- Any email correspondence sent to our contact address is used strictly to respond to your specific inquiry or feedback and is never shared with third-party marketing brokers.

---

We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect technical enhancements, regulatory changes, or advertising partner updates. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated timestamp.
