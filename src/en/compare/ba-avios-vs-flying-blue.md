# British Airways Avios vs Air France/KLM Flying Blue

## Overview

When planning a trip to Europe (or within the Americas), British Airways Executive Club (Avios) and Air France/KLM Flying Blue are two of the most accessible frequent flyer programs, as they partner with nearly every major credit card rewards system.

## Pricing Structures: Distance-based vs Dynamic

*   **British Airways Avios:** Employs a strict distance-based award chart. The price of an award flight is calculated segment-by-segment based on the actual miles flown. 
*   **Flying Blue:** Uses dynamic pricing. While they have a "starter" price for routes (e.g., US to Europe starting at 20,000 miles in economy or 50,000 in business), prices can skyrocket into the hundreds of thousands of miles depending on demand and cash prices.

## Surcharges (London vs Paris/Amsterdam)

*   **British Airways:** Infamous for exorbitant taxes and fuel surcharges. A "free" business class flight to London via BA can easily incur $700-$900 in fees each way. (Pro tip: use Avios to fly partner airlines like Aer Lingus or Iberia to mitigate these fees).
*   **Flying Blue:** Charges moderate surcharges. A business class flight to Paris or Amsterdam typically carries around $200-$300 in taxes and fees each way. While not fee-free, it is significantly more palatable than British Airways.

## Best Sweet Spots

*   **British Airways Avios:** 
    *   **Short-haul non-stop flights:** Incredible for flights under 1,151 miles on Oneworld partners (e.g., American Airlines and Alaska Airlines in the US, Qantas in Australia, JAL in Japan). 
    *   **Iberia to Spain:** Transfer Avios to Iberia Plus to book off-peak business class from the US East Coast to Madrid for just 34,000 Avios and low taxes.
*   **Flying Blue:**
    *   **Promo Rewards:** Every month, Flying Blue discounts specific routes by 25% to 50%. You can sometimes fly US to Europe in Business Class for under 40,000 miles.
    *   **Connecting Flights:** Unlike Avios (which charges per segment), Flying Blue charges based on origin and destination. Flying from New York to Rome via Paris costs the same as New York to Paris.

## Verdict

*   **Choose British Airways Avios if:** You need short, non-stop domestic flights on American Airlines/Alaska, or you plan to fly specifically to Spain using the Iberia trick. 
*   **Choose Flying Blue if:** You are flying transatlantic to Europe (especially in business class) and want to avoid the crushing surcharges of flying through London, or if you can take advantage of their monthly Promo Rewards.
