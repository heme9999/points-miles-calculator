# Marriott Bonvoy vs Hilton Honors Points Value: The Ultimate Guide

## Introduction

Choosing between Marriott Bonvoy and Hilton Honors often comes down to footprint and how you prefer to travel. But when comparing the actual *points*, which program offers the best value?

## Dynamic Pricing

Both programs have abandoned fixed award charts in favor of dynamic pricing, meaning the points required for a free night fluctuate with the cash price of the room.

*   **Hilton Honors:** Fully dynamic. A room that costs 50,000 points today might cost 80,000 tomorrow if demand spikes. However, Hilton does cap the maximum standard room reward price (often around 95,000 to 120,000 points, or 150,000 for ultra-luxury properties like Waldorf Astoria Maldives).
*   **Marriott Bonvoy:** Also fully dynamic. Marriott removed its award categories, aligning points prices much closer to cash rates. This has led to significant devaluations at high-end properties.

## 5th Night Free Rules

Both programs offer a fantastic perk for longer stays: book five consecutive award nights, and you only pay for four.

*   **Marriott Bonvoy:** "Stay for 5, Pay for 4" is available to *everyone*, regardless of elite status. The lowest-priced night out of the five is the one that's free.
*   **Hilton Honors:** The "5th Night Free" benefit is only available to Silver, Gold, or Diamond elite members. (Fortunately, holding almost any Hilton co-branded credit card grants at least Silver status).

## Value per Point

Because of how easily they are earned and the inflated pricing of rooms, hotel points are generally worth less than airline miles.

*   **Marriott Bonvoy:** Generally valued at around **0.7 to 0.8 cents per point**.
*   **Hilton Honors:** Generally valued at around **0.4 to 0.5 cents per point**.

While Hilton points are mathematically worth less, they are often much easier to earn through credit card multipliers and promotions.

## Status Benefits Impact on Value

Elite status drastically changes the math of redemptions.

*   **Hilton Gold/Diamond:** Hilton is unique in offering free breakfast (or a food and beverage credit in the US) just for having Gold status, which is easily obtainable via credit cards. This saves significant cash on award stays.
*   **Marriott Platinum:** Required for guaranteed lounge access and breakfast at most brands. Achieving Platinum requires 50 nights or specific premium credit cards.

## Verdict

*   **Choose Marriott if:** You value luxury footprints (St. Regis, Ritz-Carlton) and don't have elite status but want to utilize the 5th-night free benefit.
*   **Choose Hilton if:** You want easy access to elite perks like breakfast (via credit cards) to lower out-of-pocket costs on award stays, and you prefer earning points quickly through massive welcome bonuses.
