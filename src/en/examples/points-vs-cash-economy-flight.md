# Points vs Cash: Economy Flight Example

*Please note: This is a demonstration case study designed to show how our calculator works. It does not reflect real-time award space or real user data.*

When deciding whether to book an economy flight with cash or points, it's essential to compare the point value against the cash price, factoring in taxes and fees. Here is a typical scenario for a domestic round-trip flight.

## The Inputs

Imagine you find a flight that costs **$350** in cash. 
The airline is offering the same flight for **15,000 points** plus **$5.60** in taxes and fees.

- **Cash Price:** $350.00
- **Points Required:** 15,000
- **Taxes and Fees on Award:** $5.60

## The Calculation

To determine the value you are getting per point (Cents Per Point, or CPP), we use the following formula:

**CPP = ((Cash Price - Taxes and Fees) / Points Required) × 100**

Plugging in our numbers:
CPP = (($350.00 - $5.60) / 15,000) × 100
CPP = ($344.40 / 15,000) × 100
**CPP = 2.30 cents per point**

## The Result

In this demonstration, redeeming points yields a value of **2.30 cents per point**. Depending on the point currency (like Chase Ultimate Rewards or Amex Membership Rewards), this is generally considered an excellent redemption for economy class.
