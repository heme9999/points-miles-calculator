# Hotel Fifth Night Free Calculation Example

When redeeming hotel points, maximizing value often comes down to taking advantage of the **5th Night Free** benefit offered by programs like Marriott Bonvoy and Hilton Honors. This example demonstrates how much value you can unlock when applying this perk.

## The Scenario

Imagine you are planning a 5-night vacation at a premium resort.

*   **Cash Rate:** $300 per night
*   **Total Cash Price (5 nights):** $1,500
*   **Taxes & Resort Fees (Cash):** $300
*   **Total Cash Cost:** $1,800
*   **Points Required (Standard):** 40,000 points per night
*   **Taxes & Fees (Award):** $0 (assuming the program waives resort fees on award stays)
*   **Personal Valuation:** 0.7 cents per point

## Without the 5th Night Free

If the 5th Night Free benefit didn't exist, you would pay:
*   **Points Cost:** 5 nights × 40,000 points = 200,000 points
*   **Cents Per Point (CPP):** ($1,800 / 200,000) × 100 = **0.90 CPP**

## With the 5th Night Free

With the benefit applied, the 5th night costs 0 points:
*   **Points Cost:** 4 nights × 40,000 points = 160,000 points
*   **Cents Per Point (CPP):** ($1,800 / 160,000) × 100 = **1.125 CPP**

By saving 40,000 points, your redemption value increased from 0.90 CPP to 1.125 CPP—a significant 25% boost in value!

## Important Considerations

*   **Program Rules Vary:** Marriott offers the cheapest night free out of the 5 consecutive nights, while Hilton offers the 5th night free. Hyatt does not currently offer a global 5th-night free benefit on standard award stays.
*   **Eligibility:** You often need elite status (e.g., Hilton Silver or higher) to unlock this benefit.
*   **Not All Dates Apply:** This is just a demonstration. Actual hotel availability, cash rates, and taxes vary by date and property.
