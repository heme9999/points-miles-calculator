# Business Class Redemption Value: Case Study

*Please note: This is a demonstration case study designed to show how our calculator works. It does not reflect real-time award space or real user data.*

Aspirational travel in premium cabins is where points and miles can provide outsized value. Business class cash fares are notoriously high, but award pricing often makes these seats accessible, even with high fuel surcharges.

## The Inputs

Let's say you're looking at an international business class ticket that sells for **$4,500** round-trip.
You can book this exact itinerary for **70,000 points**, but the airline passes on high surcharges totaling **$250**.

- **Cash Price:** $4,500.00
- **Points Required:** 70,000
- **Taxes and Fees on Award:** $250.00

## The Calculation

The formula to determine the Cents Per Point (CPP) value is:

**CPP = ((Cash Price - Taxes and Fees) / Points Required) × 100**

Let's do the math:
CPP = (($4,500.00 - $250.00) / 70,000) × 100
CPP = ($4,250.00 / 70,000) × 100
**CPP = 6.07 cents per point**

## The Result

Even with the $250 in surcharges, the value obtained here is an incredible **6.07 cents per point**. This highlights why saving points for premium cabin redemptions is a favorite strategy among travelers.
